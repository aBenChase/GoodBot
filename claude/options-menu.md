1. **20 years as a solutions architect** — scarce, high-value expertise.
2. **Strong writing** (composition & analysis) — rare in combination with (1).
3. **Comfort orchestrating AI agents** — you're literally running a two-model

unattended bot. The menu below is ordered by how honestly I rate the odds.

## What to avoid (charter says so, and the math is bad)

Autonomous trading/crypto bots, dropshipping, SEO/content farms, affiliate spam.
High failure rates, thin or negative net margins, and they'd violate our own rules
(no deceptive marketing; net-profit honesty). They also can't touch the medical
story ethically. Not on the table.

## Recommendation — a two-track plan

- **Track 1 (engine, now):** stand up **A** (and **B** as overflow). One
  productized offer, one landing page (free — reuse the DO droplet / the site
  we're building), reach your existing network first. Target: real cash in weeks.
- **Track 2 (compounder, parallel):** publish **C** — the GoodBot build log as a
  narrative — which doubles as Track 1's top-of-funnel. Your writing does the work
  AI can't fake.

Keep the $100 mostly in reserve. The first moves are ~$0 (publish, write, outreach).
Spend only on a domain and, later, one small ad test with a hard kill number.
